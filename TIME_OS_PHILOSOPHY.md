# Tempo — The Unified Time OS Philosophy

> **Core Thesis**: Instead of being an isolated "task duration tracker" that users forget to open, **Tempo** is a complete, tactile **All-Around Time Application** (Alarms, Timers, Stopwatch, World Clock, and Task Calibration) brought to life by our **Chronograph Watch Mascot**.

---

## 1. Why This Makes Tempo 10× Stronger

1. **Zero-Friction Daily Habit (From Wake-Up to Wind-Down)**
   - Standalone time-tracking apps fail because users have to remember to open them before starting work.
   - When Tempo is also your **Morning Alarm**, **Quick Timer**, **Chronograph Stopwatch**, and **World Clock**, you already interact with it all day long—and every time utility seamlessly connects to **Task Time Calibration**.

2. **The Mascot Has a Literal Hardware Purpose in Every Mode**
   - Because our mascot *is* a geometric watch with physical pusher ears and watch-hand nose, every classic clock function maps directly to its anatomy:
     - **Alarms** → **Twin-Bell Morning Mascot** (dome bells + striker hammer on top).
     - **Stopwatch** → **Chronograph Mascot** (clicking the Black left ear = Lap/Split; clicking the Orange right ear = Start/Stop; watch-hand nose sweeps in real time).
     - **Timers** → **Countdown Bezel Mascot** (orange progress ring depletes around the bezel; mascot enters *Deep Focus* or *Break Chill*).
     - **World Clock** → **Timezone Mascots** (each city shows a mini mascot in the emotional state of that local hour: *Morning Twin-Bell* in New York, *Deep Focus* in London, *Sleepy/Night* in Tokyo).
     - **Task Calibration (Actual vs. Estimate)** → The core brain unifying all modes.

The market is currently fractured into **3 disconnected silos**—nobody unifies utility clock tools with personal time calibration:

| Category | Key Players | Their Strengths | Their Blind Spot |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Default OS Clocks** | **Apple Clock**, Google Clock | Pre-installed, fast utility (Alarms, World Clock, Stopwatch, Timers), Live Activities, hardware reliability. | **Zero context or memory.** A 25-minute timer in Apple Clock has no idea *what* you were doing, whether you finished early or went 15m overtime, or how your morning alarm connects to your day. |
| **2. Aesthetic / Specialized Alarms & Timers** | ** Alarmy**, **Sleep Cycle**, **MD Clock**, **Flip Clock**, **Timepage / Actions (Moleskine)** | Strong visual aesthetics (Flip/MD Clock) or aggressive wake-up mechanics (Alarmy math puzzles, Sleep Cycle tracking). | Either purely decorative desk clocks or adversarial ("punish the user to wake up") with zero connection to daytime focus or task durations. |
| **3. Time Trackers & Focus Timers** | **Toggl Track**, **Timery**, **Forest**, **Session**, **Structured**, **Tiimo** | Detailed project billing (Toggl), Pomodoro rituals (Session/Forest), visual day timelines (Structured/Tiimo). | **High daily friction & guilt.** They feel like "work surveillance" or rigid schedules. If you forget to start a Toggl timer, the app is dead to you. None of them replace your morning alarm, kitchen timer, or stopwatch. |

### B. 5 "Hidden Assumptions" of Apple Clock & Competitors (And How Tempo Breaks Them)

1. **Hidden Assumption #1: "Clock utilities are disposable—once a timer hits 00:00 or a stopwatch is reset, the time disappears forever."**
   - *Apple Clock / Competitors*: You run a 30m timer or a 42m stopwatch, tap "Stop/Reset," and that time data vanishes into thin air.
   - *Tempo's Counter-Move*: **Every timer or stopwatch run can be claimed.** When a stopwatch or timer finishes, Tempo's mascot asks: *"Want to log this 42m run to a task or keep it as a quick utility?"* Utility comes first; calibration happens effortlessly at the end.

2. **Hidden Assumption #2: "Time is purely mechanical and objective—60 minutes feels the same at 8:00 AM, 3:00 PM, and 11:00 PM."**
   - *Apple Clock / Competitors*: Treat every hour as identical sterile digits.
   - *Tempo's Counter-Move*: **Human time is elastic (Time Blindness & Energy Curves).** Tempo knows that a "20-minute task" at 9 AM takes 18 minutes, while at 5 PM it takes 38 minutes. Our Watch Mascot visually reflects both the *regime* (Twin-Bell Morning, Deep Focus Day, Inverted Night Dial) and your *personal calibration ratio*.

3. **Hidden Assumption #3: "Alarms, Timers, Stopwatch, and Tasks belong in separate apps (or completely isolated tabs that never talk to each other)."**
   - *Apple Clock*: The 4 tabs (`World Clock`, `Alarm`, `Stopwatch`, `Timer`) haven't talked to each other since iOS 1 in 2007.
   - *Tempo's Counter-Move*: **The Connected Time Chain.**
     - Dismissing your **Morning Twin-Bell Alarm** can immediately kick off a **15m Morning Routine Timer**.
     - Checking **World Clock** for London shows when your teammate wakes up *and* lets you start a **Countdown Timer** until your shared meeting window.
     - Stopping a **Stopwatch** lets you convert that split into a **Calibrated Task Estimate**.

4. **Hidden Assumption #4: "Going over your planned time = Failure (Red text, broken streaks, dead trees)."**
   - *Forest / Pomodoro / Calendar apps*: If you estimate 30 minutes and take 45 minutes, your schedule shatters or your virtual tree dies.
   - *Tempo's Counter-Move*: **Going overtime is a calibration win, not a failure.** Tempo treats estimation like a game of darts: whether you finish `-5m early` or `+12m overtime`, the mascot celebrates when you log the *truth*, because your personal Time Calibration Score gets smarter.

5. **Hidden Assumption #5: "A clock UI must either be a sterile spreadsheet OR a childish cartoon pet."**
   - *Competitors*: Either boring corporate tables (Toggl/Apple Clock) or juvenile gamification (Forest/Habitica).
   - *Tempo's Counter-Move*: **Tactile Modern Horology with Soul (Dieter Rams Braun × Living Chronograph).** The mascot *is* the watch instrument itself—its nose is the sweeping hands, its ears are the chronograph pushers/twin bells, and its bezel is the timer ring.

## 5. Build Status · Polish Pass (October 2026)

**Now working for real**

| Mode | What changed |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Alarms** | On iOS 26 alarms go through AlarmKit, so they ring like the Clock app, on the lock screen and through silent mode, with Stop and a 9 minute Snooze. On older iOS each alarm becomes a short chain of notifications with the Tempo twin-bell chime. Alarms have repeat days, can be edited by tapping a row, and the starter alarms ship switched off. |
| **Ringing screen** | When an alarm rings or a timer hits zero, a full screen takeover shows the ringing mascot with big Stop, Snooze, Done and +5 min buttons. Alarms with a chained routine offer to start it right there. |
| **Timers** | A notification with the chime fires when the countdown ends, even if the app is closed. Lock screen actions "Done & log" and "+5 min" work. Timer and stopwatch state survive the app being killed. |
| **Focus chat** | The chat composer sits at the bottom like a messaging app. The mascot answers in a speech bubble under itself, and it switches to its thinking face while you type. |
| **Stopwatch** | A visible "Save to logbook" button appears when the stopwatch is paused. |

**Bug fixed:** routine reminders used to wipe every pending notification, alarms included, each time the app opened.

**One timer everywhere.** The Focus timer is now the single source of truth. It shows on the widgets, the Live Activity and the Apple Watch. A block started from a widget, Siri or the watch turns into the Focus timer when you open the app, and a block finished there lands in the logbook. Stop and +5 min on the lock screen now move the real timer and its chime.

**Focus chat understands more.** "What's on my calendar" lists your day, and today's events show up as one-tap chips. "Ride to my next meeting" finds the meeting. "I just did email 20m" logs the block instead of starting a timer. Timer titles drop the duration, so "Design review 30m" becomes "Design review".

**Commute timers stop themselves.** Start a commute from a route answer and Tempo stops the clock when you arrive, so the real travel time gets logged.

**Settings that do what they say.** Default Duration sets the first timer. Smart Routine Suggestions and Notifications switch routine reminders off, but alarms always ring. The watch switch really stops syncing. Switches with nothing behind them and the old Clean Chat History are gone. Reset All Data also clears the timer and stopwatch.
