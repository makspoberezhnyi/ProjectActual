## The problem

Most people assume they already know how long something takes. They don't, not exactly, and not consistently. The gap between a guess and what actually happens repeats itself for years without anyone noticing, since nothing ever writes it down in a way that adds up. Actual writes it down.

## Three things worth knowing

**Built on your own history.** Not a generic average pulled from millions of other people's commutes and to do lists. Every number Actual shows you comes entirely from your own logged history, broken down by the kind of task and the situation you were in, so a normal work session and a stressed one never get blended into a single, meaningless average.

**Works without opening the app.** A widget on the home screen, a glance at the lock screen, one tap from an Apple Watch, an icon in the Mac menu bar. Starting or ending something never requires unlocking a phone or launching an app first.

**Private by design.** Your history lives in your own private iCloud account, not on a server Actual operates. Nothing is ranked, judged, or shared with anyone else unless you choose to.

## How it works

Step 1, Guess. You give a rough number before you start, fifteen minutes, two hours, whatever you honestly think.

Step 2, Live. Actual times the real thing, automatically for trips, with one tap for everything else.

Step 3, Corrected. Next time, the number you see already accounts for how long this actually tends to take you.
